About & Education

After completing my bachelor's in Statistics , I knew I wanted to take core mathematical theory out of textbooks and apply it directly to real-world operational problems. To bridge that gap, I stepped into my Master’s in Applied Data Science, expanding my toolkit from theoretical probability into end-to-end machine learning, SQL, R, and Python.

I have evolved my foundational background in theoretical statistics into practical machine learning and scalable data engineering. My work focuses on taking raw, complex datasets through the entire analytics lifecycle—from structured database management to predictive modeling and automated workflows. I am driven by applying high-dimensional analysis and mathematical rigor to convert ambiguous business challenges into clear, reliable outcomes.
