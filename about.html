About & Education

My foundation in data science was built on a rigorous Bachelor of Science in Statistics from a college affiliated with the University of Madras in Chennai. During my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong focus on primary survey design, operational research, and hypothesis testing, treating data not just as abstract numbers, but as real-world constraints.

Currently, I am pursuing my Master of Science in Applied Data Science at a different institution within Chennai. This transition allowed me to expand my core statistical roots into modern predictive modeling, high-dimensional machine learning architectures, and scalable automated pipelines.

Professional Startup Initiative
