Fibonacci Lattice-Based Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods for High-Dimensional Synthetic Financial Portfolio Generation

Abstract: Standard Monte Carlo simulations suffer from a slow rate of convergence when generating high-dimensional synthetic financial portfolios. This research paper explores advanced quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC) integration techniques utilizing low-discrepancy Fibonacci lattices.

By leveraging specific sequence properties inherent in number theory, our methodology significantly reduces variance and enhances spatial sampling efficiency in complex, multi-asset portfolio generation under non-Gaussian dependency structures.
