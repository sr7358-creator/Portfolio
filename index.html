Research & Publications

Peer-Reviewed / Conference Presentation ICCMSIS Conference

Fibonacci Lattice-Based Quasi-Monte Carlo Methods for High-Dimensional Synthetic Financial Portfolio Generation

Abstract: Standard Monte Carlo simulations suffer from slow rate of convergence ($\mathcal{O}(N^{-1/2})$) when generating high-dimensional synthetic financial portfolios. This paper explores quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC) integration using low-discrepancy Fibonacci lattices. By leveraging number-theoretic sequence properties, our methodology reduces variance and enhances sampling efficiency in multi-asset portfolio generation under non-Gaussian dependencies.
